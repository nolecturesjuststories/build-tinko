# Dal Tadka

1966

\- 1 cup toor dal

\- salt to taste

\- I tomato, chopped

\- 2 tbsp ghee

\- 1 tsp turmeric

\- 1 tsp cumin, 4 garlic, 2 red chillies
Pressure-cook dal with turmeric and salt (3 whistles). Mash, add tomato, simmer.
Heat ghee, crackle cumin, garlic and chillies, pour over the dal.

Mohan's favourite on Mondays
