# Kanda Poha

\- 2 cups thick poha

\- mustard seeds, curry leaves

\- / onion

\- turmeric, salt, sugar

\- lemon and coriander

\- peanuts

Rinse poha and drain. Fry peanuts, mustard, curry leaves and onion, add turmeric,
then poha, salt and a pinch of sugar. Lemon and coriander on top.

Meera's Sunday breakfast
