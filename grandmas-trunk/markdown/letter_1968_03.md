Goregaon , Bombay
9th March 1968

My dear Kamla,

we have shifted to Bombay. The whole office
came down by the Mail on Tuesday , files,
typewriters and all. The railway has given me a
small quarter in Goregaon, two rooms and a
kitchen, and the window looks at a coconut tree.

The sea is very big, Kamla. Yesterday evening !
walked from Dadar to the shore and stood there
Like a schoolboy. I thought of you and the
children the whole time.

Give my love to Arun and kiss little Meera for
me. Ask Amma to write. I will send the train
fare on the first of the month, and by April you
will all be here.

Yours always,

Mohan
