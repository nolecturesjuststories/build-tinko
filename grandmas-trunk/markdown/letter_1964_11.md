Dear Didi,

Our new house in Jabalpur is two rooms with a small
courtyard, and the neem tree outside gives shade all
afternoon. Mohan has planted tulsi near the door.

He works very hard in the railway office and comes home
only after seven. On Sundays we walk to the Bhedaghat
falls, it is so beautiful there.

Didi, I have some happy news which I will tell you properly
when you come for Holi. Do not tell Amma yet!

Your loving sister,
Kamla
