Dear Didi,

This Diwali I finally made the kheer the way Amma used to,
and Mohan took two helpings and asked for a third. I have
written it down on a card so it is not lost. I am keeping the
card in the trunk with our letters.

The secret is the old rice and the patience. Stir slowly, never
rush, just like Amma always said.

Arun has started school and Meera follows him everywhere.
Bombay is noisy but our neighbours are like family.

Your Kamla,

Kamla
