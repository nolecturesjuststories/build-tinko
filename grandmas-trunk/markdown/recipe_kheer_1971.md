# Kheer (the real one)

<!-- PageHeader="Diwali 1971" -->

\- 1 litre full-cream milk
\- 4 green cardamom, crushed

\- 1/4 cup old rice, washed
\- 1/2 cup sugar

\- a pinch of saffron in warm milk
\- 10 almonds, sliced

Boil the milk, add rice, keep the flame low. Stir slowly, never rush. When it coats the
spoon (about 45 minutes) add sugar, cardamom and saffron. Almonds on top.
Stir slowly, never rush! - K.
