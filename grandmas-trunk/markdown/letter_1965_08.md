My dearest Kamla,

Your telegram reached us on Wednesday and the
whole house danced! A son! Amma distributed
laddoos to the whole lane. Arun is a lovely name,
strong and bright like the morning sun.

Rest properly for forty days and do not lift
anything heavy. I am sending you a parcel with
dry fruits, two small sweaters I knitted and
Amma's old silver bowl for the baby.

I will come to Jabalpur in October to see my
nephew. Tell Mohan he must buy a camera now!

With love,
Savitri Didi
