Dada,

I passed my Twelfth with first class! Aai made shrikhand and
Papa told the whole building.

I have decided : I want to be a teacher. I have applied to the
teachers' training college in Dadar. Please tell Papa it is a good
job, he listens to you.

When are you coming home? Your bookshelf is now full of
my books.

Your sister,
Meera
