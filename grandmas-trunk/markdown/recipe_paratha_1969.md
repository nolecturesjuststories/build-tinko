# Aloo Paratha

1969

\- 2 cups wheat flour

\- coriander leaves

\- 3 boiled potatoes
\- ajwain, salt
\- ghee for roasting

\- I green chilli

Knead a soft dough. Mash potatoes with chilli, coriander, ajwain and salt. Fill, roll
gently, roast with ghee on both sides till golden spots appear.

Arun eats three!
