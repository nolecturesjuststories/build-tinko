Rail Bhavan, New Delhi
18th March 1975

Dear Kamla,

The meetings at the Railway Board end on
Friday and I will take the Frontier Mail back on
Saturday. Delhi is cold in the mornings and
very dusty .

My senior officer hinted that my name is on the
list for Head clerk. Do not tell anyone until it is
confirmed .

I bought a red cardigan for Meera and a cricket
bat for Arun from Karol Bagh . For you, a
surprise.

Yours,

Mohan
