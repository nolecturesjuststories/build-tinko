# Mango Pickle (Amma style)

May 1982

\- 2 kg raw mangoes
\- red chilli powder

\- 1 cup mustard oil
\- turmeric, salt
\- mustard and fenugreek seeds
\- a pinch of hing
Cut mangoes, dry in the sun for a day. Mix spices, add mangoes and warm oil. Keep
in the sun for a week, shake the jar every morning.

Dry jar only!
