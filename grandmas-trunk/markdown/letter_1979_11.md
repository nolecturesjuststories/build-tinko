Respected Amma ,

Today we signed the papers and Flat 6 in
Shanti Niwas is now our own. We have lived
here as tenants since 1972 and now no
landlord can ask us to leave . Forty-eight
thousand rupees, Amma, all our savings and a
loan from the railway cooperative.

Kamla cried at the registrar's office and then
laughed at herself. Arun wants a bookshelf for
his engineering books and Meera wants a
window seat .

Please come and stay with us this winter . Your
room is ready .

Your son,

Mohan
