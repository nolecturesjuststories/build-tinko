Respected Amma ,

Your granddaughter has arrived! She was born
on wednesday the 14th, just before sunset, at
the railway hospital. Kamla is well and resting,
and the baby is small but very loud .

We have named her Meera , as you wished . Arun
keeps asking when she will be able to play
cricket .

There is more news . Our whole office is being
transferred to Bombay next month. I will go
first with the office, and kamla and the
children will follow when the quarter is ready.

Your son,

Mohan
